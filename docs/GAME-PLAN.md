## Bunny hop (how it works)

You can bunny hop in two ways, and both give exactly the same result:

- **Hold Space.** You hop again the moment you land. This is the easy way.
- **Tap Space on the landing tick.** Release in the air and press again as you touch the ground. This is the timing way, and it is just as fast.

Neither way gives you extra speed by itself. Speed comes from air strafing: hold A or D while turning the mouse the same way in the air. The takeoff speed is capped at 110% of your running speed, so holding Space cannot build speed alone.
